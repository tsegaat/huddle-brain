## Goal
Create an online design course structure that increases student follow-through and completion.

## Decisions
1. **Charge students upfront for the course.**
   Why: Payment is intended to create commitment, since free courses are often left unfinished.
   Instead of: A free course with upsells, because it may attract less committed participants.

2. **Limit each cohort to 30 students.**
   Why: A small cohort keeps feedback personal and supports student progress.
   Instead of: An open self-paced course, because it would provide less personal feedback.

## Still open
- What exact price best supports commitment while matching the promised feedback.
