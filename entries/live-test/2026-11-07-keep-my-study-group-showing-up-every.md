## Goal
They want their weekly study group to keep attending past the point where turnout usually drops, around week 3. They are building a small Discord bot to help, but the real aim is sustained, willing participation.

## Decisions
1. **Rotate who hosts each week**
   Why: People reliably show up to a session they own, so giving each member a turn to run one builds commitment through ownership.
   Instead of: Relying mainly on bot nudges like RSVP polls, accountability pairs or pre-session teasers, which were seen as weaker than ownership.

2. **No points, streaks or leaderboards**
   Why: When they tried gamified attendance in another club, it felt like homework and put people off.
   Instead of: Tracking attendance streaks or a leaderboard, which was turned down based on that past experience.

3. **Send exactly one reminder, the day before the session, and never more**
   Why: They want to prompt people without nagging, keeping the bot's presence light.
   Instead of: Multiple reminders (e.g. 24 hours and 1 hour before) plus follow-up messages to people who missed a week, which was rejected as too much.
